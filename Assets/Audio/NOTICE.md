# Project-owned cue assets

The ten WAVs and author-cues.py were authored for Brutal Breakout during this implementation, not sampled from another work or copied from an OS asset. Their complete reproducible offline source is author-cues.py (Python standard library). These short, enveloped pure tones are original placeholder game feedback. There is no runtime synthesis and no third-party recording/content license. They are provided under CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . launch: rising two-tone; catch: falling two-tone; brick: short high tone; miss: low falling pair; win: rising triad; loss: falling triad. Format: mono little-endian PCM16, 22050 Hz, canonical 44-byte RIFF/WAV header. The original six cues use 0.1 second per tone. Shmup fire is a quieter falling pair (40 ms total), hit is one high tone (about 45 ms), destruction is a rising triad (about 135 ms), and player damage is a low falling pair (160 ms). Shmup game over reuses loss.wav. The generator is an authoring artifact only; no interpreter is needed for build/deployment.
